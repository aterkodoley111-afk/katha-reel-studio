## Character lock (new, Mahabharata cast in `engine/chars_mb.js`)
- **Ashwatthama**: dusky-wheat skin `#A2683F`, black hair in a high warrior top-knot with a few loose strands, thick brows, short moustache, iron-grey breastplate with gold rim, maroon dhoti `#7A1F2B`, white sacred thread, sword. **Divine mani** on his forehead: a bright cyan-white gem `#7FE7FF` set in gold, always softly glowing. In rage: eyes glow white, red-orange fire aura. After the curse: gem gone (a dark wound mark, no gore), torn grey cloth, loose hair, hunched walk.
- **Guru Dronacharya**: older, grey beard and grey top-knot, tripundra, saffron-white dhoti `#E8A24A`, rudraksha mala, big bow. Appears only in the intro and as a fading memory.
- **Arjuna**: warm skin `#C98E5E`, tall gold crown, teal dhoti `#1F7A6A`, white angavastra, gold armour, the Gandiva bow.
- **Bhagwan Krishna**: deep blue skin `#2F5DA8`, gold crown with a peacock feather, yellow pitambar, vaijayanti mala, gentle eyes, Sudarshan chakra spinning on his raised finger, gold halo. (Two arms — distinct from 4-armed Vishnu.)
- **Uttara**: lady body (same as Kunti/Radha), warm skin `#E2AE84`, plum sari `#8E3B8E` with gold border, maang tikka, hand on her belly; a soft gold glow in the womb = Parikshit.
- **Baby Parikshit**: the wrapped-baby design, no kavach, held inside a protective gold chakra glow.
- **Draupadi's five sons**: five sleeping shapes under blankets in the tent, no faces. The killing is never shown — only Ashwatthama's shadow on the tent wall, sword raised, and the diyas going out.
