## Character lock
- **Karna (adult)**: warm bronze skin `#C68650`, golden kavach (scale pattern + sun disk on chest) fused to his torso, glowing round gold kundal earrings, small gold crown band, long black hair, thin moustache, red-orange dhoti `#C8401E`, saffron angavastra, big bow. Sun-gold halo glint in divine moments.
- **Baby Karna**: wrapped in white cloth, tiny gold kavach on chest + glowing gold kundal, soft sun glow.
- **Kunti**: warm skin `#DDA77E`, royal-blue sari `#2B4C9B` with gold border, gold tiara + maang tikka, red bindi, jhumkas, long braid. Same design in every scene (young and later).
- **Rishi Durvasa**: dark ochre skin `#9C6A3E`, long white beard and white jata bun, fierce brows, tripundra, saffron robe, rudraksha mala, wooden staff + kamandal.
- **Surya Dev**: golden-orange skin `#F2A93B`, tall gold crown, red-orange robe, two lotuses, the same gold kavach + kundal as Karna, big spiky sun-ray halo.
- **Adhiratha**: brown skin `#94603C`, white turban, white dhoti + kurta, thick moustache, charioteer's reins/whip, chariot with one horse behind.
- **Radha**: brown skin `#A8704A`, green sari `#2E8B57` with gold border, bindi, simple bangles.
- **Crowd / Pandavas**: flat black silhouettes only (no new faces).
